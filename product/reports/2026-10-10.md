### Bugs / Surprises

CUDA 12.8 driver vs torch cu130 default (bernoulli pyproject pins cu130); torchaudio ABI drift (installed CPU-only variant to sidestep); AWQ loader incompatibility (transformers 5.x's gptqmodel doesn't handle Qwen2.5-VL partial quantization); vLLM 0.26 built against CUDA 13 (unusable on cu128); dtype mismatch in AWQ `lm_head`.
