### Bugs / Surprises

- **DeBERTa pipeline with batch=1 is pathological.** Not surprising in hindsight — the HF transformers pipeline warns about it (`"use a dataset for efficient batching"`) — but I undershot the fix-vs-let-it-run decision badly. Original Banking77 projection was 5-8 min; actual was 4+ hours before I caught it. Lesson: when the GPU utilization is low on a GPU-advertised workload, that's the signal to debug *first*, not later.
- **Python 3.15 released during session.** uv auto-selected it on the fresh Lambda install because `requires-python` had no upper bound. Blew up at `uv sync` because torchvision+cu130 wheels top out at cp314. Now pinned.
- **At N=500, SST-2 Bernoulli accuracy dropped from 0.95 (N=100) to 0.91** — within noise but noticeable. Generative edged ahead by 0.6pt. Honest: on classical NLP tasks DeBERTa and even Generative are competitive-to-better than Bernoulli at the dev-tier backbone. The pitch holds on calibration and on out-of-distribution content (arXiv post-cutoff), not on raw accuracy for every task.
- **The landing page previously showed `7–40×` NLL ratios.** At N=500 across the actual 9-benchmark suite, the max is 18× (CLINC150-OOS). Tightened to `7–18×` on both pages — honest adjustment.
